Choose on the below criteria : 

Range → How far devices need to communicate.

Power Consumption → Battery-powered vs. mains-powered devices.

Bandwidth → Amount of data (video vs. sensor readings).

Topology → Star (router-based) vs. Mesh (devices relay data).

Ecosystem → Compatibility with Matter, Zigbee hubs, or cloud services.

Easy Rule of Thumb
Need video or heavy data? → Wi-Fi.

Need ultra-low power? → BLE.

Need mesh for lighting/sensors? → Zigbee or Thread.

Need secure locks/alarms? → Z-Wave.

Want future-proof Matter integration? → Thread.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
Topology	Protocols	Best Use Case                                                  |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
Star	Wi-Fi, BLE, Cellular	High-bandwidth devices, wearables, cloud-heavy IoT     |
Mesh	Zigbee, Thread, Z-Wave	Smart lighting, sensors, locks, automation           |
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
